What We Do

ADAS Calibration Services

Every modern vehicle safety system requires precise calibration after repairs, replacements, or collisions. We cover them all.

Camera Calibration

Static and dynamic calibration for forward-facing, surround-view, and stereo camera systems. Required after any windshield replacement or front-end repair.

ADAS Core

Radar Calibration

Front and rear radar sensor alignment for adaptive cruise control, autonomous emergency braking, and cross-traffic alert systems.

Collision Safety

LiDAR Calibration

Precision alignment of LiDAR sensor arrays for vehicles equipped with advanced autonomous and semi-autonomous driving systems.

Advanced Systems

Windshield Recalibration

Post-replacement camera and sensor recalibration to restore full ADAS functionality after windshield glass service — often required by law.

Most Common

Lane Assist Calibration

Realignment of lane departure warning and lane-keep assist systems after suspension work, tire changes, or accidents affecting vehicle alignment.

Driver Safety

Blind Spot Monitoring

Calibration of rear-corner radar and ultrasonic sensors that power blind spot detection, rear cross-traffic alert, and parking assist systems.

Why ACE AutoLab

ADAS systems have zero tolerance for error. A misaligned sensor by even a fraction of a degree can compromise every safety feature in your vehicle.

  • Documented Results

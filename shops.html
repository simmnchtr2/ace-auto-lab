Common Questions

What Shops Ask Us First

We do serve retail customers, but partner work is kept separate. When a vehicle comes to us through your shop, it goes back to your shop. We don't solicit your customers for other services, and we don't do glass or collision work ourselves.

Most calibrations are completed the same day the vehicle arrives. Turnaround depends on the make and whether the job needs static calibration, dynamic road calibration, or both. We confirm expected timing when you send the job so you can set customer expectations accurately.

As a general rule: if the windshield was replaced, the front bumper came off, the suspension or alignment changed, or a sensor was removed, calibration is likely required. Manufacturer requirements vary considerably, so call us with the year, make, model, and what was serviced and we'll confirm before you quote the customer.

Every job returns with a pre-scan report, the calibration record showing which systems were calibrated and to what specification, and a post-scan confirming all affected systems pass. This is the paperwork insurers and adjusters typically ask for, and it's what protects your shop if a question comes up later.

No minimums and no contract. Send us one job a month or twenty. Wholesale rates are based on the account, not on hitting a quota, and there's no fee to set up or maintain a partner account.

We cover the full range of common domestic, Japanese, Korean, and European makes, including EVs. If you regularly see a specific make, mention it when you set up the account so we can confirm coverage and procedures up front.

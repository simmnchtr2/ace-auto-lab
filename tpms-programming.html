How It Works

Why New Sensors Need Programming

Each wheel has a tire pressure sensor that sends its pressure and its own ID number to the vehicle. The vehicle only listens to sensors it has learned.

When sensors are new, replaced, or moved to different wheels, they have to be programmed and relearned before the vehicle recognizes them. Until then, the TPMS light stays on or flashes, and the pressures on the dash may be missing or in the wrong spots.
